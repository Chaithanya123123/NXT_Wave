# Wheat — Agronomy Advisory

## Sowing
Optimal sowing window is mid-November to mid-December once soil temperature drops.
Use 40-50 kg seed per hectare with a seed rate adjusted for germination percentage.
Drill sowing at 20-22 cm rows outyields broadcasting by 10-15%.

## Irrigation
Critical stages: crown root initiation (20-25 days), tillering, booting, flowering and
grain filling. Water stress during flowering and grain fill sharply reduces test weight.
Stop irrigation 10-15 days before harvest.

## Nutrients
Baseline dose as per soil test; split nitrogen application — basal + one at crown root
initiation + one at first irrigation. Zinc sulphate 10 kg/ha helps on zinc-deficient soils.

## Rust Diseases
Brown rust (orange-brown pustules on leaves), yellow rust (striped yellow pustules) and
stem rust. Epidemics follow mild humid spells. Management: grow resistant varieties,
avoid late sowing, and apply triazole fungicides only when disease appears early in the
crop. Remove volunteer wheat from field bunds to break the carry-over cycle.

## Harvest
Harvest when grain moisture is around 12-14% and spikes turn golden-brown. Thresh
promptly and dry to below 12% moisture before storage to prevent mould.
