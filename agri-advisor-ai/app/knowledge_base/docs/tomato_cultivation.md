# Tomato — Cultivation Advisory

## Nursery & Transplanting
Raise nursery in raised beds; seed treatment with Trichoderma or hot-water treatment for
seed-borne diseases. Transplant 25-30 day seedlings at 60 x 45 cm spacing. Provide
temporary shade for 2-3 days after transplanting during hot weather.

## Water Management
Drip irrigation with mulch saves 40-60% water and reduces soil-borne disease splash.
Critical stages are fruit set and fruit development — alternate wetting avoids cracking
in rain-fed conditions. Avoid waterlogging: collar rot and root knot increase sharply
in stagnant water.

## Key Diseases
- **Early blight**: concentric target spots on older leaves; remove lower infected leaves,
  stake plants, rotate with non-solanaceous crops.
- **Late blight**: dark water-soaked patches in cool humid weather; destroy volunteer
  plants, avoid overhead irrigation, apply recommended contact fungicide preventively
  only during disease-favorable weather.
- **Damping-off (nursery)**: good drainage, avoid overwatering, seed treatment.

## Nutrients
Heavy feeder for potassium — high K improves firmness, colour and shelf life. Split
nitrogen to avoid lush vegetative growth that attracts whitefly and aphid.

## Harvest & Market
Pick at breaker/mature-green stage for distant markets, red-ripe for local sale.
Tomato respiration is high — pre-cool and ventilate; compare mandi modal prices for the
2 days ahead before deciding to hold or sell (price swings of 30-50% are common).
