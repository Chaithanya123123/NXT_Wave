## Groundnut Advisory
Issue: Disease management. Groundnut is susceptible to tikka disease (leaf spot) and collar rot. Apply good drainage and seed treatment with Aspergillus-based or approved bio-inoculants before sowing. At 45 DAS monitor for early leaf spot symptoms: brown-black circular spots on leaves. Avoid water stagnation which encourages collar rot. Harvest following general maturity so pods are not left wet in the field.

Issue: Harvest and storage. Harvest when 70-80% of inner shells show dark veins. Dry pods to below 9-10% moisture before storage to avoid aflatoxin development.

## Rice Advisory
Issue: Water management. Alternate wetting and drying (AWD) saves up to 25% water versus continuous flooding without affecting rice yields much. Keep a 15 cm perforated pipe to observe water level, reflood only when it drops below 12-15 cm.

Issue: Pest management for Brown Plant Hopper (BPH). BPH appears when the canopy is very dense and humidity high. Conserve natural enemies such as spiders and mirid bugs, keep bunds weeded, and drain field temporarily when hopper population approaches threshold. Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer top-dressing late in the season.

Issue: Major diseases. Blast disease shows diamond-shaped lesions on leaves; maintain field drainage and avoid very dense seeding. Sheath blight shows green-gray lesions on the sheath near water line, and is favored by high N high humidity.

## Tomato Advisory
Issue: Transplanting. Tomato seedlings are transplanted at 25-30 days after sowing. If temperatures exceed 35 degrees avoid transplanting, water seedlings twice a day. On high heat, use shade net to reduce wilting and fruit drop.

Issue: Disease. Late blight in cooler humid conditions: dark water-soaked spots on foliage that spread fast. Damping-off in nursery is prevented by seed treatment and good drainage. Never let standing water remain around the roots.

Issue: Market timing and cold storage. Tomato respires fast and loses market value in 3-4 days during summer. Harvest at green-yellow stage to sell in distant markets; compare mandi prices two days ahead to time the harvest.

## Cotton Advisory
Issue: Bollworm. Pink bollworm is managed by integrating pheromone trap monitoring (8 traps per hectare), Trichogramma releases, and timely destruction of infested squares/bolls. Avoid insecticides unless threshold counts are crossed to preserve natural enemies.

Issue: Water. Cotton needs about 6-7 irrigations; critical stages are squaring and boll development. Drip irrigation can raise yields 20-25% and reduce water use 30%.

## Onion Advisory
Issue: Bulb development. Onion is a cool season crop during first stage and needs dry conditions at maturity. On bulb formation (65-70 days) avoid watering to reduce issues with neck rot.

Issue: Storage. Curing under shade 2-3 weeks followed by storage with good ventilation reduces storage losses from 40% to under 15%.
