# Paddy — Water Management Advisory

## Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)
AWD is an irrigation practice where the field is allowed to dry between irrigations
instead of staying continuously flooded. Install a perforated water tube to field level;
refill when water drops 15 cm below the surface. AWD typically reduces water use by
20-30% with comparable yields and lowers methane emissions.

## Standing Water Depths
- Tillering: shallow 2-5 cm encourages tillers; do not keep continuous submergence.
- Panicle initiation to flowering: maintain 5-7 cm — stress here cuts grain set.
- Maturity: drain 7-10 days before harvest for easier harvest and better grain quality.

## Drainage Before Harvest
Field drainage at physiological maturity improves harvestability, reduces grain
moisture at threshing, and lowers the chance of grain discoloration.

## Fertigation / Nutrient Timing
Split nitrogen: basal 50%, tillering 25%, panicle initiation 25%. Leaf colour chart or
soil testing helps avoid over-application, which increases lodging and blast severity.

## Where AWD Is Not Advisable
On fields with very poor water-holding capacity (coarse sandy soils) or where irrigation
supply is unreliable, continuous or safe-mild flooding may still be safer.
